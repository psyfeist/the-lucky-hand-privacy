Keep both copies in > sync when the policy changes. ## Summary The Lucky Hand is a fully offline, single-player card game. It has no account system and collects no personal data. Nothing you do in the game ever leaves your device. ## What the app stores on your device Your progress is kept in files inside the app's private storage: bankroll save, lifetime statistics, and your preferences (volume, table colour, decks, screen stay on). Android backup for these files is disabled (`allowBackup=false`), they are never exported, and uninstalling the app deletes them permanently. ## What the app does not collect - No names, email addresses or account details — there is no login. - No device identifiers, advertising IDs, analytics or crash reports. - No location data, contacts, photos or microphone audio. - No usage statistics or behavioural tracking of any kind. The app requests zero Android permissions: it has no `INTERNET` permission and makes no network requests of its own. ## Third parties The app contains no advertising, no trackers and no analytics SDKs. The only link is the KO-FI button on the start menu, which opens your browser at . That site is run by Ko-fi and has its own privacy policy; the game itself sends nothing there and never sees what you do on that site. ## Distribution The game is distributed through Google Play. Google handles install and update data under Google's own privacy policy. This policy covers only what the app itself stores on your device. ## Children's privacy No personal information is collected from any user, including children — because the app collects no personal information at all. ## Changes to this policy This policy may be updated in later versions of the game. The copy inside the app is always the current one, so check there after an update. Continuing to use the game after a change means you accept the updated policy. ## Contact Questions? Tap the KO-FI button on the start menu to open and send a message there — that is the only contact channel for this game.
